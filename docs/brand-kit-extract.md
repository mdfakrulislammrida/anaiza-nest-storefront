# Anaiza Nest brand kit v1.0, extract

Essence: Affection, made visible.
Tagline: Gifted, beautifully. (always with the comma and full stop; use under the logo, on end cards, on packaging)
Supporting lines: Affection, made visible. / For the people you'd pour a second cup for. / Packed by hand, sent with care. / যত্নে সাজানো উপহার।
Promise: Every order leaves us gift-ready: beautiful to open, safe to send, easy to pay for.
One-line boilerplate: Anaiza Nest is a Dhaka gifting house for tea sets, gift boxes and homeware, packed by hand and ready to give.
Short boilerplate: Anaiza Nest curates ceramic and glass tea sets, gift collections and homeware for people who care how a gift feels to open. Every order is packed by hand and sent gift-ready, online at anaizanest.com and in store in Dhaka. Anaiza Nest is part of Fast-Signs Group, trading in Bangladesh since 2003.
Facts: Dhaka, founded 2025, sells at anaizanest.com and in store, a Fast-Signs Group concern trading since 2003.

Message pillars: 1 Gift-ready, always (arrives looking like a present; gift boxes with the product, hand packing, optional gift note). 2 Chosen, not crowded (a small edited range). 3 Made for the table (beautiful things meant for daily use, shown in use). 4 Buy with confidence (cash on delivery, a shop in Dhaka, Fast-Signs Group since 2003). Lead with 1 or 3, close with 4. Pillar 2 is for About and press.
Seal: round badge, one per layout, only three messages: Gift-ready, Packed by hand, Cash on delivery.
CTAs: Order now, pay on delivery / Find the right gift / Shop tea sets / Send a gift / Ask for a corporate quotation.

Say this, not that:
- Price: "৳1,450, gift box included."
- Low stock: "Only a few sets left in this colour."
- Sale: "Stock clearance: selected pieces at lower prices until Friday."
- Order confirmed: "Thank you. Your set is being packed by hand and will be with you soon."
- Something broke: "We are sorry your set arrived damaged. Please send us a photo and we will make it right."

Writing rules: feeling first, fact last; name the real detail; one idea per line; reassure plainly; write as the host. Sentence case, no shouting.
Words we leave out: best quality, luxury premium, exclusive, elite, cheap, hurry, grab now, limited stock!!!, world class, amazing, must-have, hot deal, wow.
Words we reach for: gift, give, pour, table, guest, by hand, boxed, ready, keep, cherish, linger, quiet, warm, glow, rim, glaze, ribbon, unwrap, thoughtful, for her, for them, for home.
Emoji: at most three per caption from 🫖 ✨ 🎁 🤍 🚚, never in a headline. Prices: ৳ before the number, comma for thousands, no decimals.
Language: English for headlines, website and corporate; Bangla script for warmth; Banglish only in private chat.

Logo: use the master file only, never redraw it. Clear space = height of the capital A on all four sides. Screen minimum 120 px wide. Profile picture uses the monogram, centred, 15% padding. One logo per layout, top left or centred. Colourways: navy #1B2A41 on ivory #F6F1E7 (default); ivory on navy (dark layouts); champagne #D4B786 on deep ink #141F30 (packaging, special occasions); one-colour black (stamps, invoices). Never stretch, recolour, place on busy photos, add effects, or shorten the name to "Anaiza".
Pour line: 1px gold line (never thicker than 2px), antique gold on ivory, champagne or gold on dark; vertical from the top edge ending in a small round drop, or a horizontal divider; may draw itself once on entering view, never loop; one per layout.
Gift frame: thin gold inner border inset 4% of the short side.
Buttons: navy fill, ivory text, 2px radius; burgundy fill for the single most important action on a page. Shadows soft and warm, products only. Icons thin single-weight line, navy or gold.
Contrast: passes = navy on ivory 12.8, burgundy on ivory 10.5, champagne on navy 7.5, stone on ivory 5.0. Avoid for text = gold on ivory 2.9, navy on burgundy 1.2. On ivory, gold is for lines, frames and large display type only; small gold text on dark switches to champagne.
Photography: four shots per product (reveal, pour, table, detail); warm side daylight; ivory, linen, pale wood or navy backdrop; true colour; no supplier photos with foreign text; no pure white cut-outs as the main image.
